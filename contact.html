03 — Good to know

Frequently asked.

Do you deliver outside Salem?

Yes. We arrange careful, insured delivery across Tamil Nadu and beyond, with white-glove placement on request.

Can I see pieces before I buy?

Absolutely. Visit our store to see finishes and fabrics in person, or book a private virtual viewing.

How long does a consultation take?

A first styling session runs for about 45 minutes, in store or online, with no obligation to purchase.

What is your returns policy?

Unused items can be returned within 14 days. Bespoke and made-to-order pieces are excluded.
