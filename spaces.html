08 — The finishing touch

Small changes.
A different feeling.

Refresh a room without starting over. A new light source, a considered textile or a rearranged favourite can shift the mood in an instant.

  • 01

    Bring in a softer light

    Use lamps at different heights to make evenings feel warm and welcoming.

  • 02

    Add a natural layer

    Introduce a tactile material that complements what is already in the room.

  • 03

    Style with restraint

    Group a few meaningful objects and leave space around them to breathe.
